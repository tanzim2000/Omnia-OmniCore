**Omnia, OmniCore, OmniView, OmniVision, OmniSync, OmniAccess,
OmniView REVIVE** and **OmniSense** are names of Tanzim Ahmed's projects.
Some are built and in use, some are planned.

The software licence (AGPL-3.0) and the licences on Resources don't give
you any rights to these names. Copyright protects the code and trademarks
protect the names; they are separate. This page says what you can do with
the names.

## You can, without asking

- Say your module, face or theme **works with**, or is **for**, OmniCore,
  as long as it's true.
- Write about the project by name: docs, articles, videos, reviews, talks.
- Run OmniCore as released, unmodified, and call it OmniCore.
- Name your own module, face or theme anything that isn't confusingly
  similar to the names above.

## You need permission to

- Use any of these names, or anything confusingly close ("OmniCore Pro",
  "Omnia Core", "OmniKore"), for your own product, fork, modified version,
  registry, company, domain or social account.
- Call a Resource "official", or say or suggest it is made, approved or
  endorsed by the project.
- Use the names in a way that could make people think your work is
  Tanzim Ahmed's.

## Modified versions

If you change OmniCore and share it, give your version a different name
and say clearly that it is modified, not the original. This is an
additional term under section 7 of the AGPL, which allows it (see
[LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md)).

## Registries

A registry that mirrors the OmniCore Resource Registry may say it is a
mirror. It may not call itself the official registry.
