# Welcome to OmniCore 2

The biggest update OmniCore has had: a new Settings, pictures and colours behind every page, frosted glass, and a city search that finds the small places too. Everything you've set up comes with you.

## What's new

- **Settings in four tabs.** General, Appearance, Resources and About, under a notch at the top. The tabs lie side by side and slide sideways, with a swipe, the Left and Right keys, or a tap on the notch. Nothing on them scrolls up and down: each tab fits your screen.
- **A picture, a video or a colour behind your pages.** On the Appearance tab, click the background preview. Pick a picture, an animated GIF, a looping video or one colour from a colour wheel. It stays on your server and is never sent anywhere. Text stays readable over it on its own, or you can set the dimming yourself.
- **Frosted glass.** Every box on OmniCore's pages blurs what's behind it, with a tint you can pick or match to your background.
- **Pages fade** from one to the next, and the background fades in once it's ready.
- **City search finds small places.** Pick which service a city search asks, with a second one when the first finds nothing. A village like Bekkestua, which the old search didn't know, is found without signing up for anything.
- **Info order lists can be dragged,** and a long one opens in a pop-up.
- **A tab icon, plain tab names,** and a pure black dark mode.

## What changes for you

- **All your settings come with you.** Faces, widgets, modules, themes, your location and your sign-in all carry over. The new settings start out looking and working as before, so nothing needs setting up again.
- **Light mode looks different.** It's a warm Beige Cream now, instead of light grey. Dark mode is black, as before.
- **Updates live on the About tab** now. The old Updates page address still works and opens it.
- **Your modules and themes keep working.** Nothing they use has changed.
- **You'll sign in again** after the upgrade, as after any update.
- **A new licence.** OmniCore 2 is free software under the GNU AGPL-3.0. Nothing changes in how you use it at home. What it means in plain words is in [LICENSING.md](https://github.com/tanzim2000/Omnia-OmniCore/blob/main/LICENSING.md).

## Why upgrade

New work happens on OmniCore 2. Version 1 stays where it is: it keeps running, but new features and new modules are made for 2.

## When you press Upgrade

It takes a few minutes, mostly downloading. Your screens go blank for a moment while OmniCore restarts, and Settings follows it all the way and tells you when it's done. If OmniCore 2 doesn't start properly, the version you have now comes back on its own. What happens to your data and your `docker-compose.yml` is just below.
